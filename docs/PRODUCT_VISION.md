# RUNWAY: Immersive Third-Person World

Authority: the user's Master Product + Architecture Reorientation, September 19, 2026, supersedes earlier first-person, fixed-camera, primitives-only and no-character-selection constraints. Earlier documents remain historical context. High-quality immersive 3D is a product requirement, not optional decoration.

## Experience

A visible human inhabits a continuous browser-rendered world. Walk/run from a furnished interior through a doorway into a courtyard/street, vegetation and a pond shoreline, with coherent architecture and distant scenery. Interact with people and objects through proximity, clicks and the existing validated voice choices. Story panels are contextual interfaces, not the default activity behind which exploration is hidden.

The GTA reference describes spatial freedom and embodied interaction, not a promise of native AAA fidelity, unlimited scale, or permission to use proprietary assets. Push browser quality using authored assets, stable ecosystem libraries and offline processing rather than reducing the world to primitive mannequins or static panorama plates.

## Preserve

The startup story, five-event demo, 35-encounter campaign, saved progress, sound/mute, real Devin mission, independent verification and LIVE/CACHED/MOCK distinctions remain useful. No graphics task may fabricate engineering evidence or expose provider credentials.

## Immediate slice

One continuous apartment/workspace-to-courtyard-to-pond route; rigged human locomotion, third-person camera collision, separate physics geometry, indoor/outdoor lighting, PBR surfaces, vegetation, water, atmosphere and quality controls. Two genuinely distinct selectable models are desirable; do not present recolors as distinct humans or a seated GLB as a locomotion-ready character.

## Non-goals before acceptance

City-scale content, vehicles, crowds, new paid integrations and a production WebGPU migration. Phase 4 expansion follows a visually convincing, profiled vertical slice, not merely mounted canvas pixels.

Status: foundation implementation on `feat/third-person-foundation`. Prior first-person release is preserved on main. See ROADMAP.md for evidence gates; the full target is not yet implemented.
